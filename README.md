FetalAI: Using Machine Learning To Predict And Monitor Fetal Health
Project Description :

"FetalAI" employs machine learning to predict and monitor fetal health during pregnancy. By analyzing various parameters from medical records and real-time monitoring devices, the system predicts potential complications and monitors fetal well-being. This proactive approach aids healthcare providers in early intervention and personalized care, improving pregnancy outcomes.

Scenario 1:Early Intervention:

Healthcare providers use FetalAI to detect early signs of fetal distress, enabling timely interventions and reducing the risk of complications during pregnancy and childbirth.



Scenario 2:Remote Monitoring:

Pregnant individuals utilize FetalAI for remote fetal health monitoring, allowing them to track their baby's well-being from the comfort of their homes, reducing the need for frequent clinic visits and enhancing convenience.



Scenario 3:Risk Prediction:  

FetalAI predicts the risk of preterm birth or other pregnancy complications based on individual health data, enabling healthcare providers to implement preventive measures and tailor care plans for high-risk pregnancies, ultimately improving maternal and fetal outcomes.
